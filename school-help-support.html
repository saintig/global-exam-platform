Help & Support

Everything you need to run examinations and results on SAINTIG EXAMS, step by step.

Start here

Follow these ten steps in order. Tap any step to jump to its guide.

  1. Set up your school profile, then add your subjects and students.
  2. Register an examination and add its questions.
  3. Publish it and share the Exam ID with your students.
  4. Create a class for the term.
  5. Attach examinations and choose which students take each one. Check the examination register and set a limit if you need one.
  6. Enter CA scores (optional).
  7. Generate the draft result and check it carefully.
  8. Publish and lock it, then share the Class ID.
  9. Repeat for each term.
  10. At the end of the year, combine the terms into an Annual Result.
CA is optional. If your school does not use Continuous Assessment (CA), skip the CA step. Results then come from the examination scores only. If a page asks for the CA maximum, enter 0.

The four IDs you will use

Exam IDCreated when you save an examination. Students use it to take the exam or check that exam's result.
Student IDBelongs to one student. It is always entered together with one of the other IDs.
Class IDCreated with each class. Students use it to see their published class result.
Annual Result IDCreated when you generate the annual result. Students use it to see their annual result.

Students enter these IDs on the SAINTIG EXAMS homepage, in the Take Your School Exam box. Viewing and printing results is free for students.

Part 1: Subjects, students, examinations and questions

How to register everything an examination needs.

1Prepare your school details

When you create a class (see Part 2) you enter the school name, phone, email, address, logo link, the class teacher's name and the principal's name.

These details are printed at the top of result sheets and report cards. If a name is left empty, a blank line is printed so it can be written by hand.

Open My Profile to check your account details.

2Register your subjects
  1. Open School Subjects.
  2. Add each subject you teach.
  3. Make sure each subject is published.

Only published, active subjects appear when you register an examination. Archived subjects are hidden.

3Register your students
  1. Open School Students.
  2. Add each student.
  3. Every student has a Student ID. Give each student their own ID privately.

Only active students can take exams and appear in classes. Archived students are hidden everywhere.

4Register an examination

Open Register Examination. It has four steps shown at the top of the page: Exam, Add questions, Review & save and Publish.

Step 1: Exam details

  • Subject: choose one of your published subjects.
  • Title: for example "Government Examination".
  • Exam name and class (optional): for example "Government SS1".
  • Instructions (optional): shown to students.
  • Duration: minutes allowed. Your package sets the maximum.
  • Questions each student answers: how many questions every student receives. Your package sets the maximum.
  • Marks per question and Maximum attempts (1 to 5). The total marks are worked out for you.
  • Start and end dates (optional): tick the box only if students must take it in a set period.

Tap Save and continue. The Exam ID is created.

Step 2: Add questions

Upload a file (PDF, Word, Text, CSV or Excel), or type one question at a time. See Preparing your question file.

Step 3: Review and save

Check the questions. Edit or delete any, then tap Save all questions.

Step 4: Publish

See Publish and share the Exam ID.

You can leave and come back. Pick your unfinished examination from the list at the top of step 1.

5Prepare your question file

Any normal file name works, for example Government SS1 Questions.csv, First Term Exam 2026.xlsx or My Questions (Final).CSV. Only the ending must be .pdf, .docx, .txt, .csv, .xlsx or .xls.

Word, Text and PDF

Write one item per line, with a blank line between questions:

1. What is government?
A. The study of plants
B. The process of governing a country
C. The study of animals
D. The study of rocks
Answer: B
Explanation: Government is how a country is run.
Marks: 1

2. Who heads a state government?
A. Governor
B. Senator
C. Chairman
D. Principal
Answer: A
  • Start each question with a number such as 1. or 1).
  • Type the letters A. B. C. D. yourself. Word's automatic lists are not read.
  • Every question needs an Answer: line with one letter.
  • Explanation and Marks are optional.
  • PDF files must contain real text. Scanned or photographed pages cannot be read.

CSV and Excel

Put these column names in the first row. Their order does not matter:

question_text, option_a, option_b, option_c, option_d, correct_option, explanation, marks
  • Required: the question, the four options and the correct option. The rest are optional.
  • Excel is read from the first sheet. CSV can use commas or semicolons.
  • The page has a Download CSV template button and a Download Word / Text sample button.

If some rows have problems, the good questions are still accepted. The problem rows are listed with their row or question number so you can fix them and read the file again.

6Your question limits

Your package sets three limits:

  • The longest an examination can be (minutes).
  • The most questions one student can answer in an examination.
  • The total number of active questions you can keep.

Example: your package allows 20 active questions, and each student answers 10.

  • You upload 50 questions to one examination. The first 20 are kept and the other 30 are discarded. You are told this on screen.
  • You publish it. Each student gets a different random 10 of the 20.
  • For your next examination you cannot add questions, because the 20 places are used. Delete or archive questions you no longer need to free places, or ask the admin to upgrade your package.

Archived questions do not count towards the limit. The page shows how many places you have left.

7Publish and share the Exam ID
  1. Go to step 4, Publish.
  2. Publishing works when the saved questions are at least the number each student answers.
  3. Tap Publish examination. The Exam ID is shown, with Copy ID and Share ID buttons.
  4. Send the Exam ID to your students. Give each student their own Student ID privately.

After publishing, questions can no longer be added or changed. Check everything first.

8How students take the exam
  1. The student opens the SAINTIG EXAMS homepage.
  2. In Take Your School Exam, they stay on the Take Exam tab and enter the Exam ID and their Student ID.
  3. They check the exam details and tap Start.
  • Each student gets a random set of questions, in a random order. Answer options keep their order.
  • A timer runs. If a student leaves and comes back before time ends, they continue with the same questions.
  • Students can attempt the exam only as many times as you allowed.

Part 2: Classes, examinations and results

How to set up a class, share IDs and publish results.

1Create a class
  1. Open Class Examination.
  2. Tap Create Class.
  3. Fill in the school details (name, phone, email, address, logo link), the class name (for example SS1), the class arm if you have one (for example A), the academic session (for example 2026/2027) and the academic period (term or semester). Add the class teacher's and principal's names.
  4. Tap Save Class. The class gets a Class ID, shown in the list of classes.

Create a separate class for each term, using the same name, arm and session. This is how the annual result later combines the terms.

The Classes tab lets you view, edit, deactivate or archive a class. Archived classes can be restored.

2Add students to the class
  1. On Class Examination, tap Add Students.
  2. Choose the class, then the student. Students already in that class are not listed.
  3. Repeat for each student.

Students must already be registered in School Students. The Students tab lists them, and you can deactivate or remove a student from the class without deleting the student.

3Attach examinations and choose who takes each one
  1. On Class Examination, tap Attach Examination.
  2. Choose the class, then the examination. Examinations already attached to that class are not listed.
  3. Open the Examinations tab. For each attached examination, tap its Students button.
  4. By default, every student in the class is registered for a newly attached examination. Under Registered, select the students who do not take it and tap Remove Selected. The list then belongs to that examination only.
  5. To add students again, use the Add Students tab and tap Add Selected.

In the same window you can print or download that examination's student list.

A student who is in the class but registered for no examination is left out of the result. The Class Results page warns you when this happens.

4The examination register: lists with IDs

Use the register to see, check and share who is registered for what in a class.

  1. On Class Examination, tap Open Examination Register, or tap Register on a class in the Classes tab.
  2. Choose the class.
  3. By examination: every examination in the class with its Examination ID, and the students with their Student IDs under each one.
  4. By student: every student with the examinations they take, and each Examination ID.

What you can do

  • View: tap "View students" or "View examinations" on any card. Use the search box to find an exam, student or ID.
  • Edit: "Edit students" on an examination, or "Edit examinations" on a student, to change who takes what.
  • Print: print the whole register, or just one examination or one student.
  • Download: save a CSV file that opens in Excel, for everything or for one examination or student.
  • Share: send the list by WhatsApp or any app, or copy it. Share a student's list with the student or parent. Share student IDs only with school staff or the student concerned.

In the Students tab, the Exams button beside a student opens that student's examinations straight away.

5Maximum examinations per student

You can set the most examinations each student may take in a class, for example 8.

  1. Open the Examination Register and choose the class.
  2. Under Maximum examinations each student can take in this class, type a number and tap Save limit. Tap Remove limit to have no limit.
  • When you add students to an examination, students who already take the maximum are skipped, and you are told who.
  • In the register, students over the limit are marked "over limit".
  • When you attach a new examination, every student is registered for it by default, so some students may go over the limit. A message tells you, and you can fix it by removing those students from the examination.

The limit is saved with your account, so it appears on every device you sign in with. It is set for each class separately.

6Examinations not attached to the class are not counted

A student can write any examination you publish, using the Exam ID and their Student ID, whether or not it is attached to the class.

Class results count only the examinations attached to the class and only the students registered for each one. An examination the student wrote but that is not attached to the class, or one the student is not registered for, is not included in the class result.

To include it: attach the examination to the class, then make sure the student is registered for it.

7Enter CA scores (optional)

CA means Continuous Assessment, such as tests and assignments. You can skip this step.

  1. Open Class Results and select the class.
  2. On the CA Scores tab, choose an examination.
  3. Enter the CA maximum for that examination, for example 30. Use 0 if there is no CA.
  4. Type each student's CA score. A blank box counts as 0. A score cannot be above the maximum.
  5. Tap Save CA Scores.

Each student's total for an examination is their exam score plus their CA score. CA scores cannot be changed while a published result is locked.

8Generate and check the draft result
  1. On Class Results, open the General Result tab.
  2. Tap Generate Draft.
  3. Check every student, score, total, average, grade and position.
  • The result combines every examination attached to the class.
  • A student who missed an examination counts as 0 for it.
  • Students with equal totals share a position.
  • You can tap Regenerate Draft as often as you like before publishing.
  • The Examination Result tab shows the result of just one examination.

Students cannot see a draft. Printouts of a draft are marked "DRAFT — NOT PUBLISHED".

9Publish and lock the result
  1. When you are sure the draft is correct, tap Publish and Lock Class Results and confirm.
  2. Students can now see and print their result.

While a result is published, it is locked. It cannot be edited, and CA scores cannot be changed.

10Share the Class ID
  1. The Class ID is shown on the Class Results page when you select the class.
  2. Send it to your students. They also need their own Student ID.
  3. Students open the homepage, choose the Class Results tab in the Take Your School Exam box, and enter the Class ID and Student ID.

To share the result of a single examination, students use the Exam Results tab with the Exam ID and their Student ID.

11If you must unpublish a result

Tap Unpublish and Unlock Class Results. Students lose access straight away.

The unpublished result is permanently archived. It cannot be edited, restored or published again, but you can still view and print it. To give students a result again, generate a new draft, check it and publish it.

12Grading scale

On Class Results, the Grading Scale tab sets the grades. A score gets the highest grade whose minimum percentage it reaches.

  • Edit, add or remove grades, then tap Save Grading Scale.
  • Use Default Scale goes back to the standard scale.
  • Changes apply to results you generate from then on. Published results keep their grades.
13Print result sheets and report cards
  • Print Class Result Sheet: one sheet for the whole class.
  • Print All Report Cards: one report card per student. Use the Card button beside a student to print just that card.

If nothing opens, allow pop-ups for the site in your browser, then try again.

14Annual results
  1. Publish and lock the class result of each term first.
  2. Open Annual Results and select the class and session.
  3. Tick the terms to combine. A term can only be used when its class result is published and locked. Tick at least two.
  4. Tap Generate Annual Draft and check it.
  5. Tap Publish and Lock Annual Results.
  6. Share the Annual Result ID (use Copy ID or Share ID). Students enter it with their Student ID in the Annual Results tab on the homepage.

Annual results work like class results: the draft can be regenerated, and unpublishing permanently archives that version.

Archived classes do not appear in the list, so archive a term's class only after you have finished with it.

For students: what to tell them

You can copy this and send it to your students.

?Student instructions
  1. Open the SAINTIG EXAMS website and find the Take Your School Exam box.
  2. To take an exam: stay on Take Exam, enter the Exam ID and your Student ID, then tap Take Exam.
  3. To see an exam result: choose Exam Results, enter the Exam ID and your Student ID.
  4. To see your class result: choose Class Results, enter the Class ID and your Student ID.
  5. To see your annual result: choose Annual Results, enter the Annual Result ID and your Student ID.

Results can be viewed and printed for free once your school has published them. Keep your Student ID private.

Packages and upgrades

What your package controls and how to change it.

?About packages
  • Packages are Free, Silver, Gold and Custom. Each sets limits on students, subjects, examinations, stored questions, questions per exam and exam length.
  • Open Subscription Plans to see your package and what each one allows.
  • To upgrade: contact the admin and tell us the package you want. You can pay manually. Once your payment is confirmed, your new package goes live and you get a notification.
  • Custom package: if Silver and Gold do not suit your school, ask for a Custom package built around your needs.

Problems and quick answers

Tap a problem to see the fix.

My file will not upload, or some questions were skipped
  • Read the list shown under Read file. It names the row or question and what is wrong, for example an empty option or a missing answer.
  • Fix those rows in your file and read it again. Questions that were fine are not lost.
  • Check the ending is .pdf, .docx, .txt, .csv, .xlsx or .xls.
  • For Word and Text files, type the numbers and letters yourself. See Prepare your question file.
  • Word, PDF and Excel files need an internet connection to be read.
"You have reached your package's limit"

You have used all your active question places. Delete or archive questions you no longer need, or ask the admin to upgrade your package. See Your question limits.

I cannot publish my examination
  • Saved questions must be at least the number each student answers. Save more questions, or lower "Questions each student answers" in step 1.
  • Questions still in the review list must be saved first.
  • A published examination cannot be published again.
A student says the exam is not found
  • Check the Exam ID and Student ID are typed correctly.
  • Check the examination is published and not archived.
  • Check the student is active and not archived.
  • Check any start and end dates you set.
A student cannot start again

The student has used all the attempts you allowed. You can allow more attempts in the examination's settings if your page lets you change this.

A student is missing from the result

Check the student is active, is in the class, and was chosen for the examinations with the Students button. See Attach examinations.

A student wrote an examination but it is missing from the class result

Class results count only examinations attached to the class and students registered for them. See Examinations not attached to the class are not counted.

A student could not be added to an examination

The student may already take the maximum number of examinations you set for the class. Check the limit, then remove the student from another examination or raise the limit.

Students cannot see their class or annual result
  • The result must be published and locked. A draft is not visible to students.
  • Check they use the right ID: Class ID for class results, Annual Result ID for annual results.
A class, student, subject or examination is missing from a list

Archived items are hidden from lists and selection boxes. Check Archive Management.

Printing does not open

Allow pop-ups for the site in your browser settings, then tap the print button again.

I published a result by mistake

Unpublish it, then generate, check and publish a new one. Read If you must unpublish a result first: the unpublished version is permanently archived.
