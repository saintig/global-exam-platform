Step-by-step guides for Teacher's Examination and School Class Examinations.

Start here

The dashboard has two tabs. Choose the one that matches how you work.

Teacher's ExaminationYour own students, subjects, examinations and questions. Students here can write every examination in this tab. They need a Student ID and an Exam ID. Guide: Teacher's Examination.
School Class ExaminationsClasses with three terms each, with their own students, subjects and examinations. A student writes only the examinations attached to them. Includes class and annual results, promotion and graduation. Guide: School Class Examinations.
The two tabs are separate. Students, subjects, examinations and questions you create in one tab never appear in the other. A Teacher student cannot write a class examination, and a class student cannot write a Teacher examination. The dashboard numbers change with the tab. Your package limits count both tabs together.

Teacher's Examination in 4 steps

  1. Add your subjects and students.
  2. Register an examination and add its questions.
  3. Publish it and share the Exam ID with your students.
  4. Give each student their own Student ID. Students take the exam on the homepage.

School Class Examinations in 8 steps

  1. Create the class. Its three terms are made for you.
  2. Add the subjects of the class and term.
  3. Add the students of the class.
  4. Create examinations and their questions, then publish.
  5. Attach examinations and students to each other, and set a limit if you need one.
  6. Enter CA scores (optional) and generate the draft result.
  7. Publish the result and share the Class ID.
  8. Repeat for each term. At the end of the year, make the annual result and promote the class.

The IDs you will use

Exam IDCreated when you save an examination. Used with a Student ID to take the exam or see that exam's result.
Student IDGiven to one student when you register them. Used together with the other IDs.
Class IDOne for each term of a class. Students use it to see their class result.
Annual Result IDCreated when you generate the annual result.

Students enter these IDs on the SAINTIG EXAMS homepage, in the Take Your School Exam box. Viewing and printing results is free for students.

Teacher's Examination

For a teacher or a school that wants simple examinations without classes.

1Add your subjects
  1. Open My Subjects and tap Add Subject.
  2. Type the subject name and save. It is published automatically.

You can edit, publish or unpublish, archive and restore a subject, and see the examinations linked to it. A subject name can be used once in this tab.

2Register your students
  1. Open My Students and tap Add Student.
  2. Type the full name. The Student ID is created for you.

Use Copy ID or Share beside a student, and Print All, Download All or Share All for the whole list. Give each student their own ID privately.

Only active students can take examinations. A student with results cannot be deleted. Archive the student instead.

3Register an examination and add questions

Open Register Examination. It has four steps: Exam, Add questions, Review & save and Publish.

  • Subject: one of your published subjects.
  • Title and instructions for students.
  • Duration, questions each student answers, marks per question and maximum attempts. Your package sets the maximums, shown under each box.
  • Start and end dates are optional.

Tap Save and continue to create the Exam ID. Then upload a file or type questions one at a time. See Preparing your question file.

4Prepare your question file

Any file name works. Only the ending matters: .pdf, .docx, .txt, .csv, .xlsx or .xls.

Word, Text and PDF

1. What is government?
A. The study of plants
B. The process of governing a country
C. The study of animals
D. The study of rocks
Answer: B
Explanation: Government is how a country is run.
Marks: 1

2. Next question…
  • Start each question with a number such as 1. and type A. to D. yourself. Word's automatic lists are not read.
  • Every question needs an Answer: line with one letter. PDF files need real text, not scans.

CSV and Excel

Use these columns in the first row. The page has Download CSV template and Download Word / Text sample buttons.

question_text, option_a, option_b, option_c, option_d, correct_option, explanation, marks, question_order, image_filename

If some rows have problems, the good questions are still accepted and the problem rows are listed so you can fix them.

5Your package limits

Your package sets: the longest examination, the most questions one student answers, the most attempts per student, and the total number of active questions you can keep, for both tabs together.

If you upload more questions than you have places for, the first ones are kept, the rest are discarded, and you are told. Archived questions do not count.

6Publish and share the Exam ID
  1. Go to step 4, Publish. The saved questions must be at least the number each student answers.
  2. Tap Publish examination. Use Copy ID or Share ID.

After publishing, questions cannot be added or changed. Check everything first. To change a published examination, unpublish it in the Question Bank.

7Question Bank

Filter by subject and examination, search the questions, and use View Questions, Edit, Publish, Unpublish, Archive and Delete on any examination or question. If attempts exist, Delete archives instead, so results are kept.

8How Teacher students take the exam
  1. Open the homepage and find Take Your School Exam.
  2. Stay on Take Exam, enter the Exam ID and the Student ID.
  3. Check the details and tap Start.

Each student gets a random set of questions. A timer runs, and a student who leaves can continue until time ends. Attempts are limited to what you allowed.

School Class Examinations

Classes with three terms, their own students, subjects and examinations, results, promotion and graduation.

1Create a class
  1. Open Classes & Terms and tap Create Class.
  2. Choose the class from the list: Basic 1 — Primary 1 up to Basic 9 — JSS 3, then SS 1 — Grade 10, SS 2 — Grade 11 and SS 3 — Grade 12.
  3. Add the arm (optional), the academic session (for example 2026/2027), the school name, class teacher and principal. Contact details and logo link are optional.
  4. Tap Create Class and Terms. The 1st, 2nd and 3rd Term are created together, each with its own Class ID.

Each class card has View, Edit, Promote or Graduate, Archive and Delete, and quick buttons to open the class's Subjects, Students, Examinations and Attach pages.

Delete works only for a class that holds nothing. Otherwise archive it.

2Add subjects to a class and term
  1. Open Class Subjects.
  2. Choose the Class and the Term at the top.
  3. Tap Add Subject.

Subjects belong to one class and term, so the same name (for example Mathematics) can be used in each term and class. Subjects never appear in the Teacher tab.

3Add the students of a class
  1. Open Class Students and choose the Class.
  2. Tap Add Student for one, or Add Many Students to paste one full name per line.

Each student gets a Student ID and is added to all three terms of the class. The list with the new IDs is shown so you can print or share it. These students are new and separate from your Teacher students.

Each student also has an Attach Exams button that opens the attach page for that student.

4Create examinations and questions
  1. Open Class Examinations and choose the Class and Term.
  2. Follow the four steps: details, questions, review and save, publish.

The subject list shows only the subjects of that class and term. Questions are prepared exactly as in Preparing your question file. View and edit them in Class Question Bank.

A new class examination starts with no students. You choose who writes it in the next step.

5Attach examinations and students to each other

A student writes only the examinations attached to them, and an examination opens only for the students attached to it. You can attach either way, many at once.

  1. Open Attach Students & Examinations and choose the Class and Term.
  2. Examinations tab: tap Attach students beside an examination, tick the students (use search and Select all shown), then Attach selected.
  3. Students tab: tap Attach exams beside a student, tick the examinations, then Attach selected.
  4. To undo, tap Remove on either tab and tick what to remove.

Each row also has View, Print and Download. At the top, Print all, Download all and Share all cover the whole class term. Attach buttons also appear on Class Students and in the Class Question Bank.

A student who is in the class but attached to no examination is left out of the class result.

6Lists with IDs: view, print, download and share

On the attach page you can see every examination with its Exam ID and the students with their Student IDs, or each student with their examinations.

  • View: the list for one student or examination.
  • Print and Download (a CSV file for Excel): one row or everything.
  • Share all: send the list by WhatsApp or copy it. Share Student IDs only with school staff or the student concerned.
7Maximum examinations per student

On the attach page, type a number under Most examinations each student can write in this term and tap Save limit. Remove limit clears it. Each term has its own limit.

  • A student can write fewer than the limit, but not more.
  • When you attach students, any who already have the maximum are skipped and you are told how many.
8Who can write which examination
  • A class student can write only class examinations attached to that student.
  • A Teacher student can write only Teacher's Examination examinations.
  • A class student cannot write a Teacher examination, and a Teacher student cannot write a class examination.

Class results and annual results

Open Class Results and choose the class and term.

1Enter CA scores (optional)

CA means Continuous Assessment. You can skip it, and use 0 for the CA maximum.

  1. On CA Scores, choose an examination and enter its CA maximum, for example 30.
  2. Type each student's CA score. A blank counts as 0. Tap Save CA Scores.

A student's total is the exam score plus the CA score. CA cannot be changed while a published result is locked.

2Generate and check the draft
  1. On General Result, tap Generate Draft.
  2. Check every score, total, average, grade and position.

The result combines every examination attached to the class term. A student who missed an examination counts as 0 for it. You can regenerate the draft as often as you like. Students cannot see a draft.

3Publish and lock the result

Tap Publish and Lock Class Results and confirm. Students can then see and print their result.

While a result is published, it is locked and cannot be edited.

4Share the Class ID
  1. The Class ID is shown on the Class Results page.
  2. Students open the homepage, choose Class Results in the Take Your School Exam box, and enter the Class ID and their Student ID.

For one examination only, students use Exam Results with the Exam ID and their Student ID.

5If you must unpublish a result

Tap Unpublish and Unlock Class Results. Students lose access straight away.

The unpublished result is permanently archived. You can still view and print it, but it cannot be edited or published again. Generate a new draft to publish again.

6Grading scale and printing
  • Grading Scale sets the grades. Use Default Scale returns to the standard one. Published results keep their grades.
  • Print Class Result Sheet prints one sheet for the class. Print All Report Cards prints one card per student, and Card prints a single card.

If nothing opens, allow pop-ups for the site in your browser.

7Annual results
  1. Publish and lock the class result of each term first.
  2. Open Annual Results, choose the class and session, and tick at least two terms.
  3. Tap Generate Annual Draft, check it, then Publish and Lock Annual Results.
  4. Share the Annual Result ID. Students use it with their Student ID in the Annual Results tab on the homepage.

Promotion, graduation and archive

What to do at the end of a year, and how archiving works.

1Promote a class
  1. Open Classes & Terms and tap Promote on the class.
  2. Enter the new academic session (and the arm and teacher if they change).
  3. Tap Promote Class. A new class is created at the next level with its three terms, and the students move into it.
  • The earlier class, with its subjects, examinations and results, stays under Previous classes (promoted).
  • In the new class, create the subjects and examinations again, then attach students and examinations.
  • If you already have a class at the next level, promote that class first. A different session keeps the two apart.

Which classes can move: Basic 1 to 5, Basic 7 to 9 and SS 1 to SS 2 are promoted. Basic 9 (JSS 3) is promoted to SS 1. Basic 6 — Primary 6 can be promoted to Basic 7 — JSS 1 or graduated. SS 3 graduates.

2Graduate a class

Tap Graduate (or Promote / Graduate for Basic 6). The class moves to Graduated classes with all its history. It cannot be edited. To change it, tap Move back to return it to Active classes.

3Undo a promotion

Under Previous classes, tap Demote back. The students return to the old class, and the class it was promoted to is removed.

This works only if the new class has no subjects, examinations, results or students that were not in the old class. Remove or archive those first.

4Archive and restore
  • Archiving a class archives everything under it too: its students, subjects, examinations and questions, even published ones. Archived records are not counted in your numbers or package capacity. A notice tells you how many were archived.
  • Restoring a class brings back only the class. Its content stays archived until you restore it in Archive Management, in order: the class first, then subjects (which bring back their examinations and questions), then students.
  • On the Teacher side, archiving a subject archives its examinations and questions, and archiving an examination archives its questions.

You cannot restore a subject, examination, question or student while its class is archived. You are told to restore the class first.

For students: what to tell them

You can copy this and send it to your students.

?Student instructions
  1. Open the SAINTIG EXAMS website and find the Take Your School Exam box.
  2. To take an exam: enter the Exam ID and your Student ID.
  3. To see an exam result: choose Exam Results, enter the Exam ID and your Student ID.
  4. To see your class result: choose Class Results, enter the Class ID and your Student ID.
  5. To see your annual result: choose Annual Results, enter the Annual Result ID and your Student ID.

Keep your Student ID private. If a message says the exam is not attached to your ID, ask your school to attach it.

Packages and upgrades

?About packages
  • Packages are Free, Silver, Gold and Custom. Each sets limits on students, subjects, examinations, stored questions, questions per exam, exam length and attempts. The limits count both tabs together.
  • Open Subscription Plans to see your package. To upgrade, contact the admin. When payment is confirmed your new package goes live and you get a notification.
  • Custom package: ask for one built around your school if Silver and Gold do not suit.
  • When a paid package ends: your account returns to Free, and your subjects, students, examinations and questions move to Archive with a notice. Results can still be viewed and printed. Subscribe again to restore them.

Problems and quick answers

Tap a problem to see the fix.

"Exam ID not found" or "Student ID not found"
  • Check the ID is typed correctly. Check the examination is published and not archived, and the student is active and not archived.
  • The Student ID must belong to the same school as the examination.
"This examination has not been attached to your Student ID"

Class examinations open only for students attached to them. Open Attach Students & Examinations and attach the student. See Attach examinations and students.

"This Student ID belongs to Teacher's Examination" (or School Class Examinations)

The student and the examination are in different tabs. A Teacher student writes only Teacher examinations, and a class student writes only class examinations attached to them. See Who can write which examination.

"Restore the class first"

The class of that record is archived. Restore the class in Classes & Terms, then restore the record in Archive Management. See Archive and restore.

My file will not upload, or some questions were skipped

Read the list under Read file. It names the row and the problem. Fix those rows and read the file again. See Prepare your question file.

"You have reached your package's limit"

Delete or archive records you no longer need, or ask the admin to upgrade your package. Limits count both tabs together.

I cannot publish my examination

Saved questions must be at least the number each student answers, and questions waiting in the review list must be saved first.

A student is missing from a result, or could not be attached
  • The student must be active, in the class, and attached to the examination.
  • The student may already take the maximum number of examinations you set. Check the limit.
Students cannot see their class or annual result

The result must be published and locked, and they must use the Class ID or Annual Result ID with their Student ID.

Something is missing from a list

Archived items are hidden. Check Archive Management, and check you chose the right class and term at the top of the page.

Printing does not open

Allow pop-ups for the site in your browser, then try again.
