About Our Platform

About SAINTIG EXAMS

Transforming learning and examination through technology.

SAINTIG EXAMS is a digital examination and learning platform developed to make academic assessment easier, more accessible and more organised for students, teachers and schools.

Our platform provides students with opportunities to practise, prepare for important examinations, test their knowledge and monitor their academic progress. It also provides teachers and schools with digital tools to create and manage examinations, organise question banks, conduct online assessments, monitor student performance and manage examination results.

Whether you are a student preparing for an important examination or a Teacher/School account looking for a better way to organise assessments, SAINTIG EXAMS is designed to support your educational needs.

Our Mission

To improve access to quality examination practice and modern digital assessment tools by making examination management and learning more efficient, accessible and convenient.

Our Vision

To become a trusted digital examination platform connecting students, teachers and educational institutions across Nigeria and beyond.

Ready to Begin?

Whether you are a student ready to practise or a teacher or school ready to organise examinations, SAINTIG EXAMS is here to support your digital examination journey.

The Digital Advantage

A Better Way to Conduct Examinations

Discover how digital examination systems can help reduce some of the challenges associated with traditional paper exams.

Traditional Paper Exams

  • ✕Expensive printing and photocopying costs.
  • ✕Teachers spend considerable time marking scripts.
  • ✕Risk of human errors during marking.
  • ✕Risk of examination malpractice and question leakage.
  • ✕Delayed results for students and parents.

The SAINTIG EXAMS Advantage

  • ✓Help reduce printing and photocopying expenses.
  • ✓Automatic marking for supported objective examinations.
  • ✓Consistent automated scoring based on approved answers.
  • ✓Controlled examination access and question management.
  • ✓Faster result processing and access after release.

Actual results depend on the examination setup, available features and the security procedures used by each school.
