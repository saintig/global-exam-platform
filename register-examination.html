📘 How to prepare your exam file: guideline for Word (DOCX), Text, PDF, CSV and Excel

1. Word (.docx), Text (.txt) and PDF

Type each question in this order, one item per line, with a blank line between questions:

1. What is government?
A. The study of plants
B. The process of governing a country
C. The study of animals
D. The study of rocks
Answer: B
Explanation: Government deals with the governing of a country.
Marks: 1

2. Who is the head of a state government?
A. Governor
B. Senator
C. Chairman
D. Principal
Answer: A
  • Question: start with a number, such as 1., 1) or Q1.
  • Options: exactly four lines starting A. B. C. D. Type the letters yourself.
  • Answer: required, one letter from A to D, for example Answer: B or Ans: B.
  • Explanation and Marks are optional. If you leave Marks out, the examination's "Marks Per Question" is used.
  • In Word: type the numbers and letters yourself. Word's automatic numbered or lettered lists are not read. Tables, text boxes and pictures inside the document are ignored.
  • PDF: must contain real text. Scanned or photographed pages cannot be read.

2. CSV (.csv) and Excel (.xlsx, .xls)

Use the template exactly, with headings in the first row:

question_text, option_a, option_b, option_c, option_d, correct_option, explanation, marks, question_order

Excel files are read from the first sheet. Question order is set automatically when the questions are saved.

3. Filenames

Any normal filename works, for example Government SS1 Questions.csv, First Term Exam 2026.xlsx or My Questions (Final).CSV. Spaces, brackets and capital letters are fine. Only the ending must be .pdf, .docx, .txt, .csv, .xlsx or .xls.

4. After choosing your file

Package limit: your package allows a set number of stored questions in total. If a file has more questions than you have room for, only the questions that fit are added and approved automatically, and the rest are skipped. The queue section shows how many places you have left.

Tap Read Questions. If there are errors, the message names the row or question number to fix. Good questions go to the review queue, where you can edit and approve them before saving.
