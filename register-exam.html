Register Examination

Create examinations, add questions, upload images and manage publication.
Important: Saving questions does not publish the examination. Questions are stored under the selected examination. Students can access them only after the examination is published.

1. Examination Details

Free: 45 minutes. Premium: 90 minutes.

2. Select an Existing Examination

Select an unpublished examination if you want to continue adding questions to it.

3. Add Individual Questions

Questions must have four options (A–D), one correct option, and valid marks. Images are optional. Maximum image size: 5 MB.
Maximum 5 MB. The image will be uploaded to the exam-images bucket.

4. Bulk Question Upload (CSV)

Upload a CSV file containing multiple questions. You can download the prepared template below. For image questions, upload the images separately and enter their exact filenames in the image_filename column.
Image filenames must match the CSV image_filename values. Each image must be 5 MB or smaller.

5. Question Upload Queue

Review your questions before saving them. The queue is temporary and will be cleared when you leave or refresh this page.

Saved questions: 0

6. Publish Examination to Students

Publication requirements:
Free examinations require at least 50 saved questions.
Premium examinations require at least 100 saved questions.
Publishing questions does not automatically publish the examination.
Select an examination to view its publication status.
