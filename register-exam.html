Register Examination

Create examinations, add questions, upload images and manage publication.
Important: Free and Premium examinations are separate. Each examination has its own question collection. Saving questions does not publish the examination to students.

1. Examination Details

Premium is stored as "paid" in the database.
Free: 45 minutes. Premium: 90 minutes.

2. Select an Existing Examination

Select an examination to continue working on its draft or view its publication status. Free and Premium examinations are clearly identified.

3. Add Individual Questions

Questions are saved only to the selected examination. All four options are required. Images are optional. Maximum image size: 5 MB.
Maximum 5 MB. Images use the exam-images storage bucket.

4. Bulk Question Upload (CSV)

Upload multiple questions using the exact 10-column CSV template. For image questions, upload the images separately and enter their exact filenames in the image_filename column. Each image must be 5 MB or smaller.
Each image must be 5 MB or smaller.

5. Question Upload Queue

Review questions before saving them. The queue is temporary and will be cleared if you refresh or leave this page.
Questions in queue: 0
Saved questions: 0

6. Publish Examination to Students

Publication requirements:
Free examinations require at least 50 saved questions and use 45 minutes.
Premium examinations require at least 100 saved questions and use 90 minutes.
Publishing questions does not automatically publish the examination.
Select an examination to view its publication status.
