Important: This is the initial dashboard interface. User management, subscriptions, visitor tracking and editing forms must be connected to secure backend operations before they become functional.

Countries & Education

Manage countries, education systems, levels and classes.

This section will connect to the existing education tables after their relationships and permissions have been checked.

Subject Management

Add and maintain subjects for each education system.

The subject management form will be connected to the existing subjects table in the next implementation step.

Examination Management

Create examinations, configure access and manage publication.

Examination creation must be connected to the existing examinations table and its subject relationships.

Question Bank

Create and maintain multiple-choice questions.

The question form will support question text, options A–D, correct answer, explanation, marks and image/diagram uploads using secured storage.

Students & Teachers

Review registered accounts and account activity.

User listing and account actions will be implemented using secure, authorized backend operations.

Paid Subscribers

Review subscription plans, payment status and expiry.

A subscription data source and verified payment records are required before real subscriber figures can be displayed.

Visitor Analytics

Review daily, weekly, monthly and yearly visitor activity.

Visitor counts require a visitor-tracking system. Registered users and examination attempts are not a substitute for visitor analytics.

Results & Attempts

Review examination activity and student performance.

Result access will be connected to the existing examination-attempt tables after permissions and scoring rules are verified.

Platform Settings

Manage platform-wide access and configuration.

Settings that affect security, registration or examination access must be enforced on the backend, not just in this page.
