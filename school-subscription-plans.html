How to subscribe

Silver and Gold can be paid for online with Paystack. Choose how many days you want (30 days, 60 days and so on) and your package is activated as soon as the payment is confirmed. If you pay while your package is still active, the new days are added after your current end date.

The Custom Package is arranged with the administrator and activated by the administrator only.
