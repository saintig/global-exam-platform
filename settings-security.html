Prices and Benefits

Prices are per month (30 days). When a school pays for several months online, the amount is the monthly price × the number of months, from 1 to 12 months, where 12 months is 365 days. Leave a price empty to show “Price to be announced”.

One benefit per line. Up to 12 lines of 120 characters each. The numbers (students, subjects and so on) are added automatically from the Package Limits.

Stop a School's Package

Return a Teacher/School account to the Free package at any time, even before its days run out. Nothing is deleted. The school keeps its data but cannot add more until it is within the Free limits.

Send Notification to Teachers/Schools

Messages appear on the school's Notifications page and on its 🔔 bell.

To give one school a Silver, Gold or Custom package with its own days and limits, use Registered Users (coming next).
